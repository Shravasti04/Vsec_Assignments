Admissions

Admissions are conducted according to the rules and regulations of the Government of Maharashtra and the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE).

Eligibility

  • Passed 12th Science
  • Qualified MHT-CET / JEE Main
  • Valid CAP Registration
  • Required documents available

Required Documents

  • SSC Marksheet
  • HSC Marksheet
  • MHT-CET/JEE Score Card
  • Leaving Certificate
  • Aadhar Card
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport Size Photographs

Admission Process

  1. Register through CAP Portal.
  2. Fill the college preference form.
  3. Verify documents.
  4. Seat allotment.
  5. Report to the institute.
  6. Pay admission fees.
  7. Confirm admission.

Important Notice

Admission dates, fee structure and seat availability are updated every academic year. Students should regularly check the college website for official notifications.
