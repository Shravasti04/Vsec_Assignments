About Our College

Karmaveer Adv. Baburao Ganpatrao Thakare College of Engineering (KBTCOE), Nashik, is one of the leading engineering colleges in Maharashtra. The institute offers quality technical education, experienced faculty, modern laboratories, library facilities, and excellent placement opportunities.

Vision

To become a center of excellence in engineering education by developing skilled professionals with ethical values and innovative thinking.

Mission

  • Provide quality technical education.
  • Promote research and innovation.
  • Develop leadership and teamwork skills.
  • Encourage lifelong learning.

Principal's Message
